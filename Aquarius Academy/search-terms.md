## Expansion rules

- Add a term only when a post found with a related phrase has a read, and its likes/views beat the niche median after at least 4 reads exist. Write the date and the counts under ## Added.
- Retire a term after 3 searches in a row that returned nothing on lane. Move it to ## Retired with the date and the miss count.

- Never replace the lane.
