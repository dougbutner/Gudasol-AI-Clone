Privacy Policy

Gudasol AI Clone · Effective August 27, 2026 · Last updated August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Gudasol AI Clone (the “Project”), operated in connection with Gudasol (Douglas James Butner), handles information when you use the repository, local tools, generated media, or related publishing workflows.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to people who clone or run this Project, publish media created with its assets, or otherwise interact with the tools and documentation in this repository. It does not apply to third-party platforms (YouTube, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Pinterest, Nango, or similar) except as described below.

2. Information we do not collect

The Project is primarily a local toolkit. The repository and default scripts do not operate a central account system, do not sell personal data, and do not run analytics or advertising trackers as part of the Project itself.

We do not intentionally collect:

  • names, emails, or account credentials for a Gudasol AI Clone user database;
  • payment information;
  • precise location;
  • biometric templates (face images in the repo are likeness assets, not a biometric identification service).

3. Information processed on your machine

When you run the tools locally, processing typically happens on your computer. That may include:

  • source images, audio, video, and generated clips stored in folders you choose;
  • configuration files such as .env and connection JSON (API keys, tokens, page IDs);
  • logs, caches, and model files created by local software.

You are responsible for securing those files. Tokens and secrets must not be committed to public repositories.

4. Third-party services

If you connect publishing or AI services, those providers process data under their policies. Depending on how you configure the tools, that may include:

  • video files, titles, descriptions, tags, and privacy settings sent to social platforms;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens stored on your machine or with an auth broker (for example Nango);
  • prompts, media, and metadata sent to any AI or cloud APIs you choose to use.

We do not control those services. Review their privacy policies before connecting accounts. Do not upload other people’s personal data without a lawful basis and their permission.

5. Generated media and likeness

Outputs that use Gudasol’s likeness are subject to the Project license and the Terms of Service. Publishing that media on your channels may disclose that the work is AI-generated and not an official Gudasol release, as required by those terms.

6. Children

The Project is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction). Do not use the tools to create, collect, or publish personal information about children.

7. Retention

The Project does not operate a hosted user-data store. Retention of local files, tokens, and published posts is under your control and the policies of the platforms you use.

8. Your choices

You may:

  • stop using the tools and delete local copies of assets, outputs, and secrets;
  • revoke third-party OAuth tokens in each platform’s security settings;
  • take down content you published, subject to each platform’s rules.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated in the repository. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when material revisions are made. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or the Gudasol likeness license should be directed to Gudasol (Douglas James Butner) through the official project repository or other channels Gudasol publishes for this Project.

This document is provided with the Gudasol AI Clone project and is not a substitute for legal advice.
